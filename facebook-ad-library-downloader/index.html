Facebook Ad Library Downloader (Free, HD)

Grabowl FADS is a free browser extension that adds a Download HD button to every ad in the Facebook Ad Library. Save an ad's video in full HD with one click — or download every ad on screen at once.

Add to Firefox — free

Why Grabowl FADS

  • Always HD — the player shows SD by default; Grabowl grabs the real HD file.
  • Bulk download — every ad on the results page in one click.
  • All ad formats — video, image and carousel ads.
  • Free — no account, no watermark, no login, no limits.
Perfect for competitor ad research. Build a swipe file of winning creatives in seconds.

How to download videos from the Facebook Ad Library

  1. Install Grabowl FADS.
  2. Open facebook.com/ads/library and search a brand or keyword.
  3. Click Download HD on any ad, or Download all on screen.
