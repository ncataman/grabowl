Frequently asked questions

How do I download videos from the Facebook Ad Library?

Install Grabowl FADS, open facebook.com/ads/library, and a “Download HD” button appears on every ad. Click it, or use “Download all on screen” to grab the whole page at once.

Does it download Facebook ads in HD?

Yes. The player shows SD by default, but Grabowl reads the real HD video link, so every download is the full-quality file.

Can I download Instagram reels, stories and photos?

Yes. Grabowl adds a download button inside Instagram and saves posts, reels, stories and carousels at original quality — or a whole profile at once.

Is it free? Do I need an account?

It is completely free with no account, no watermark, no login and no limits.

Is it safe / private?

Yes. Grabowl collects nothing — no analytics, no servers. See the privacy policy.
