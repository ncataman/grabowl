# Grabowl Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 19 August 2026_

## Summary

Grabowl collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry, and no
server belonging to this extension. Media you download travels from Instagram's
servers to your computer and nowhere else.

## What the extension accesses, and why

- **instagram.com pages.** The extension reads the media data Instagram sends to
  your browser so it can offer a download button and find the original-quality
  file. This happens entirely inside your browser.
- **Instagram's CDN (`cdninstagram.com`, `fbcdn.net`).** Download URLs point here.
  Your browser fetches the file exactly as it would if you saved it manually.
- **Your Instagram session.** When the extension needs details about a post that
  it has not already seen, it asks Instagram for that post using the session you
  are already logged in with. It never reads, stores, or transmits your
  credentials or session cookies.

## What is stored

Only your own settings — the filename pattern, download concurrency, bulk limits
and the zip preference — using the browser's extension storage. If you have
browser sync enabled, your browser may sync those settings between your own
devices. Nothing else is stored, and nothing is stored outside your browser.

## What is transmitted

Nothing. No usage data, no download counts, no page addresses, no identifiers.
The extension has no backend to send anything to.

Aggregate install and active-user counts shown to the developer come from the
Chrome Web Store, Microsoft Edge Add-ons and Firefox Add-ons dashboards. Those
are produced by the stores themselves and contain no information about individual
users or their activity.

## Third parties

None. The extension bundles all of its code; nothing is loaded from a remote
server at runtime.

## Your responsibility for downloaded content

Downloaded media remains the property of whoever created it. Use it in line with
Instagram's terms and applicable copyright law — personal archiving is not the
same as redistribution.

## Not affiliated with Instagram

Grabowl is an independent project. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or
sponsored by Instagram or Meta Platforms, Inc.
